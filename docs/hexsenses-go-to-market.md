## Offers (win–win–win)

### Self-serve product
| Tier | Price | Promise |
|------|--------|---------|
| Invite / Free | $0 | Capped runs for testers |
| Pro | ~US$20–30/mo | Everyday research + Projects |
| **Max (hero mid tier)** | **~US$100/mo** | Higher limits, deeper runs — ChatGPT Pro–comfortable ceiling for consumers |
| Team (later) | Seat or flat | Shared projects |

Do **not** push consumer plans much above $100; above that use retainers.

### Services (main lever to $10k profit without 150 subscribers)
| Offer | Price | Delivery |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Research retainer | US$500–2,000/mo | You run Projects for them (angles, scripts, competitors) |
| Video pack | US$300–800 / pack | 4–8 shorts/ads via existing video tools — research brief from Hexsenses |

### Partners (not MLM)
- **Referral:** credit or **20–30% recurring** on Pro/Max they bring (paid subscription only).
- **Agency:** freelancers / salon marketers sell outcomes; they keep service margin, you keep software (+ optional pack fulfillment).
- **No** “earn by recruiting downlines.” Earn only by **paying customers** or **delivered client work**.

---

## Path to ~US$10k/month profit (example mixes)

**A — Retainers-heavy:** 6 × $1,200 + a few video packs ≈ $7k–10k+ revenue; keep LLM/tools lean.  
**B — Product-heavy:** ~120–150 × $100 Max after cost (slower).  
**C — Hybrid (preferred):** a few retainers + Max subscribers + packs.

**Backup plans (resilience):** multiple offers (Pro, Max, retainer, packs); fair-use caps on expensive models; Studio/credits only after research cash is stable.
